The challenge

Healthcare documentation shouldn't slow down healthcare.

Home visits can produce important details across languages and people. A voice-first register helps bring those details together for review.

01 / ENTRY

Manual documentation

Visit notes can take time to enter while the worker is moving between households.

02 / LANGUAGE

Natural language

Workers may speak in Hindi, Marathi, English, or mixed speech—not in form fields.

03 / PEOPLE

Multiple patients

One spoken note may mention more than one person, each needing a separate record.

04 / REVIEW

Important details

Age, Hb, BP, weight, and symptoms should be visible, reviewable, and traceable.

One note, separate records

Multiple people stay distinct.

The extraction step can turn one spoken note into separate review cards. Each record gets its own quote evidence and configured rule evaluation.

“Kamla Devi ka Hb 8.5 hai. Aur Suresh, 62 saal ke hain, BP 160/100 hai.”

Patient 1 · Kamla Devi

Hb: 8.5 · “Hb 8.5 hai”

REFER SOON · configured Hb rule

Patient 2 · Suresh

Age: 62 years · BP: 160/100

URGENT · configured BP rule
Trust through clear roles

AI understands the note.
Rules keep risk logic visible.

The model extracts what was said. Deterministic Python code applies the configured thresholds and symptom keywords.

Sarvam AI

  • Transcribes the spoken note
  • Extracts patient fields and quotes
  • Reads the reviewed record aloud
→

Deterministic Python rules

  • Parses configured BP and Hb values
  • Checks configured danger-sign phrases
  • Returns urgent / refer soon / none with reasons

The LLM does not assign the risk category. Prototype thresholds need clinical review and do not replace clinical judgment.
